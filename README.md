### CHEM-000490: Experimental verification of tunneling-driven XeF₆ pseudorotation
- **Subfield:** Round 6
- **Open:** Separate quantum tunneling from residual thermal motion in low-temperature XeF₆ pseudorotation. Identify predicted splittings or isotope-sensitive exchange in a characterized environment.
- **Status:** Sedgi and Kozuch predicted rapid near-zero-temperature fluorine tunneling in 2020. The 2025 polyfluoride study still cites XeF₆ as a computational prediction.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Tests quantum dynamics in a classic fluxional hypervalent molecule.
- **Key works:** I. Sedgi and S. Kozuch (2020), “Heavy atom tunnelling on XeF6 pseudorotation,” [DOI: 10.1039/D0CP03205K](https://doi.org/10.1039/D0CP03205K); C. Müller et al.
